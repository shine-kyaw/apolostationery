Payment

Cash on delivery. Pay the delivery person when your package arrives at your door.

Prepaid banking. You can also pay in advance by bank transfer.

Returns

If your product is damaged, defective, incorrect or incomplete when it is delivered, file a return request within 14 days of the delivery date. For selected categories we also accept a change of mind.

To be accepted for return, a product must:

  • be unused, unworn, unwashed and without flaws;
  • include its original tags and packaging;
  • be returned in the original, undamaged manufacturer packaging or box.

To start a return, call 09 777 271 999 or see all contact details.
